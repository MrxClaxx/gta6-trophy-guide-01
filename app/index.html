    Missable Watch

    Positive Mental Attitude — the only trophy that may be missable

    What it asks: meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida (10 locations, with Jason and Lucia).

    Why it can be missed: the first meditation spot is offered at home in Chapter 1 and becomes permanently unavailable after that chapter. Nobody has confirmed whether the trophy needs it, but PowerPyx earned the trophy after doing it — so do it to be safe.

    The good news: the last meditation needs Jason and Lucia playable together, which opens after Chapter 2 — and 100% Completion only asks for 15 of the 18 spots, so this trophy cannot cost you A Long Road. Everything else, story trophies included, cannot be missed.

    Source: PowerPyx via GTA Zone, Nov 18 2026.

    Save strategy — no mission replay, no New Game+

    1. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter (15 slots are available).
    2. Make one more manual save just before the final mission.
    3. Do the Chapter 1 meditation spot when it is offered at home — do not skip it.
    4. After the story, free roam stays open for everything else, so cleanup cannot be missed.

    Cheats disable trophies — and saving

    GTA VI has 61 cheats, and none of them work before the credits: each one unlocks after the story, with its own requirement on top. Using any cheat disables trophies, achievements and saving (GTABase says the block lasts for the current session, as it did in GTA 5 and RDR2). Save before you cheat, and keep your trophy runs cheat-free.

    Source: PowerPyx and GTABase via GTA Zone, Nov 18 2026.
